NINETEEN PEOPLE

PERSONAL SHIFT LOG — OPERATION AMANECER

19 people in the terminal. 12 seats available.

Captain Arendt says the transport can't wait any longer. I asked him to send me the order in writing. He told me he has no time for formalities. I told him he'd find the time if he had to explain to seven families why we were leaving them here. He cut the connection.

There's a woman with two children. One of them keeps asking when they'll have breakfast. We have enough food for about six hours, but no one dares open the emergency containers. The doctor isn't responding.

I asked for volunteers to stay behind. Nobody raised a hand.

Of course nobody raised a hand. I don't know what I was thinking.

The captain is calling again. He says I have five minutes left.

I'm not going to choose. Let him come here and do it himself.
