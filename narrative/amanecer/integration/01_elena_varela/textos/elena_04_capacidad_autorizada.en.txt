AUTHORIZED CAPACITY

PERSONAL ARCHIVE — E. VARELA
Date: Unknown

I've found the technical report for the sixth transport. I don't know how it got here. Someone left it at my terminal. No sender.

I've checked it three times.

Authorized evacuation capacity: 24 passengers.

Twenty-four.

Arendt told me there were twelve seats. He repeated it three times. Twelve seats, Elena. We can't take anyone else. There were nineteen of us.

I've spent seventeen years trying to remember the faces of the seven people we left in that terminal. I've thought about them every time I've watched a ship depart. I've written their names on scraps of paper and then burned them.

And now it turns out we could have taken them all.

I've tried to contact Arendt. His file says he died nine years ago. I called Salcedo. No answer. I looked up the second authorization on the manifest.

It was signed by someone who wasn't listed among the crew.

I don't recognize the name. But I recognize the identification number.

It belongs to one of the seven people we left behind.
