HATCH H3

MAINTENANCE LOG — E. VARELA
Date: 17 years after Operation AMANECER

Hatch H3 is jamming again. I've requested a replacement four times. Today they told me the model has been discontinued. They offered to install a second-generation pneumatic lock, but they'd have to dismantle the entire frame. I told them to leave it as it is.

It's the same hatch we used during the evacuation of Liria. Operation AMANECER. Three days without sleep, six transports, and twenty thousand forms nobody had time to fill out.

Marta, from maintenance, says I should apply for early retirement. I explained that would be difficult, given that I haven't turned fifty yet. She laughed.

Today I found one of those yellow tags under the access panel. BOARDING PRIORITY. I remember we ran out on the second day and had to write the numbers by hand.

I also remember a man who refused to wear his. Said he wasn't livestock.

I don't remember his name. Or that's what I tell myself.

E. Varela
