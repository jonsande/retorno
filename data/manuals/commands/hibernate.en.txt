hibernate until_arrival
hibernate <years>

Purpose
- Advance long-duration transit time with reduced output noise.

Requirements (gating)
- `until_arrival` requires active local transit or a valid nominal physical arrival horizon.
- Blocked when `life_support` is degraded enough to forbid hibernation.
- Blocked in critical power quality / brownout conditions.

Operational behavior
- `until_arrival`: sleeps until current transit completes, stopping at physical rest.
- `<years>`: sleeps target duration in large chunks and continues ordinary time after arrival if more was requested.
- Physical phase ends and ordinary electrical, health, radiation, job, and narrative boundaries are coordinated without one-second loops. A genuinely unsupported boundary stops the wait and reports executed time; remaining time is not silently skipped.
- Wakes on critical events that require operator attention and on a physical stop outside the destination.
- While in transit, hibernation also wakes when ambient radiation crosses the configured threshold (`HIBERNATE_WAKE_ENV_RAD_THRESHOLD_RAD_PER_S`).
- In Textual, the simulation displays elapsed game time while it runs. Commands become available again after wakeup.

Related commands
- `nav <node_id>`, `nav abort`, `status`, `alerts`
