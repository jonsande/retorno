Description
- Location targets accept the compact public ID shown by contacts/maps; TAB offers legitimate contextual targets. Full archived IDs remain valid. See: man navigation.

- `nav map sectors`: list known world sectors and their sector coordinates.
- `nav map galaxy [scale]`: show galactic map at selected scale (`sector=10ly`, `local=100ly`, `regional=10000ly`, `global=500000ly`) using known nodes + current position.
- `nav map graph`: show the global graph of known nodes/routes.
- `nav map graph <node_id>`: focus graph output around one known node.
- `nav map path <node_id>`: compute the best known path from current node.
- `nav map routes`: list operational direct routes from current node.
- `nav map contacts`: list known contacts with sector, region, and route status relative to current node.
- `nav map contacts sector`: group known contacts by sector and order sectors by proximity.
- `nav <node_id>`: start transit to destination.
- `nav --no-cruise <node_id>`: start transit without forcing CRUISE profile.
- `nav abort`: stop active travel at the exact current position, creating a temporary waypoint. It does not return the ship to its origin.
- At a stopped waypoint you can remain, use ship systems, save/load and `scan` with normal requirements.
- Use `route solve <node_id>` and then `nav <node_id>` to reach a valid third destination within sensor range and 45 ly; returning to the origin is optional.
- Routes back to the origin or onward to the previous destination still require real travel.
- The waypoint remains during departure travel; arrival elsewhere or another abort clears its temporary references while retaining historical intel.

Dependencies and gating
- `nav map *`: depends only on `core_os`.
- `nav <node_id>` and `nav --no-cruise <node_id>`: depend on `core_os` plus a valid reachable travel context.
- Hard travel hop cap: any single hop above `MAX_ROUTE_HOP_LY` is blocked (default `45ly`).
- `core_os` state impact:
  - `NOMINAL/UPGRADED`: full control.
  - `LIMITED`: blocks `uplink`, `route solve`, `auth recover`.
  - `DAMAGED`: blocks the above and also `nav <node_id>` + `nav --no-cruise <node_id>`.
  - `CRITICAL`: emergency recovery set only.
  - `OFFLINE`: minimal survival terminal only.

Propulsion and fuel
- engines speed selects an interstellar target: max follows the current ceiling; explicit 0.1c steps remain fixed. Default ceilings are 0.5c/0.4c/0.3c for nominal/LIMITED/DAMAGED, or 0.8c/0.6c/0.4c with engine_speed_upgrade_01. The selected speed multiplies fuel cost; see man engine_speed.
- Interstellar speed and ETA are fixed on departure, including the speed used by onboard clocks. Subsequent wear, repair or module changes affect future departures. Local travel remains at 20 km/s.
- status and diag engines show available and actual transit speed. Route listings and confirmation show estimated galactic duration.
- Requires powered engines at DAMAGED or better. CRUISE preserves their electrical auxiliaries.
- nav map routes shows reserve and the cruise target once, then a compact Fuel/Speed/ETA line per known direct route. Capsule estimates are approximate (~) to three decimals. Local speeds use km/s and compact durations. Unavailable estimates show a dash; brief notices mark insufficient fuel or blocked departure.
- Departure confirmation shows the public destination ID, target/effective cruise, galactic ETA, capsule cost and estimated remaining reserve. Displayed estimates use ~ and three decimals; calculations, checks and the eventual debit retain full precision. Local confirmation shows 20 km/s, compact ETA and zero capsules. The ordinary question is neutral; drone-abandonment warnings remain. Cancellation does not start travel or charge capsules; acceptance revalidates current conditions.
- Accepted interstellar departure charges once; insufficient antimatter leaves navigation unchanged.
- Local travel costs no capsules. nav abort gives no refund and adds no charge.
- A new voyage carries 12.0 equivalent capsules, including fractional contents. Wear increases cost; repair and engine_efficiency_upgrade_01 improve efficiency. See man engines.

- Speed may be queried during transit but configured only afterward. Fixed targets invalidated by damage/removal block the next interstellar departure; local travel ignores the target. Target speed is independent of the CRUISE electrical profile.
