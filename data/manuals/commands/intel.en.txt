intel
- Lists known navigation intel (latest first).
Purpose
- Manage navigation intelligence records (inspect, import, export).

Usage: intel | intel <amount> | intel all
- `intel` shows the 20 most recent records.
- `intel <amount>` shows that many most recent records, or all available when fewer exist.
- `intel all` shows the complete history.
- Lists are ordered newest first. For equal timestamps, the record inserted later appears first.
- Columns:
  ID: intel item identifier.
  kind: intel type (node/link/sector/coord).
  what: short description of the target.
  conf: confidence (0.00–1.00).
  source: origin of the intel (may include @ref).
  age: time since recorded.
- Intel kinds:
  node: a known contact or an unresolved reference (e.g., HARBOR_12).
  sector: a known sector identifier (e.g., S+001_-002_+000).
  link: a reported connection between two nodes (A -> B); the record alone does not authorize travel.
  coord: exact coordinates (x,y,z) that point to a nav point.

intel show <intel_id>
- Shows details and provenance for a specific intel item.

intel import <path>
- Imports navigation intel from a file.
- Supported markers: NODE:, LINK:, SECTOR:, COORD:
- NODE resolves a registered ID, name or alias. An absent destination remains a clue; it does not become a station.
- SECTOR provides regional information without identifying every contact. COORD identifies a navigation point, not a station.
- Optional blocks:
  [INTEL] ... [/INTEL]
  - A LINK becomes an operational route only when both endpoints are resolved and the hop is at most 45 ly.
  - An unresolved or longer LINK remains a contact and historical record; it does not authorize travel.
  - Import a canonical SECTOR first when its destinations still need to be materialized, then reimport the LINK.
  - If it contains a registered node_id, name, alias or sector id, the matching contact can be identified.
  - Unresolved tokens remain uncertain records. Corrupt intel may fail or identify a preexisting hub within range.
- Files under /data/nav, /mail or /logs may auto-import on cat.

intel export <path>
- Exports current intel to a file under /logs/nav or /data/nav.

Related commands
- Installation references use compact public IDs, also accepted by navigation commands. Retained navigation archives keep their original references and integrity data. See: man navigation.

- `uplink`, `nav map routes`, `route solve <node_id>`, `cat <path>`
