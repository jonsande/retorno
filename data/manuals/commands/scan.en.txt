scan

Purpose
- Perform local signal scan and update nearby contact intelligence.

Requirements (gating)
- Sensor stack must be available for scan workflow (`sensors` state and `sensord` service).
- Power state must allow active scanning operations.

Operational behavior
- Queues a scan job with a timed active sweep.
- After `nav abort`, a stopped waypoint is a normal scan origin: range is measured from that position and no transit scan module is required.
- Active travel keeps its existing scan restrictions; this does not authorize scanning during a journey.
- On completion, updates local contact awareness and may reveal detection events.
- If new contacts are discovered, it emits `signal_detected` and its mapped cue.
- If operating context changes before completion, the job may be interrupted.
- A completed sweep records its position, conditions and detections; the latest 128 observations are retained without forgetting contacts.
- A no-signals result applies to that observation and does not confirm that space is empty.
- Detecting only known contacts is reported separately from detecting no signals.
- An interrupted or cancelled sweep records no completed result; memory adds no requirement to repeat scans to unlock exploration.

Related commands
- `boot sensord`, `diag sensors`, `jobs`, `nav map contacts`, `route solve <node_id>`
