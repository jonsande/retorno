cargo
cargo audit

Purpose
- Inspect cargo manifest and synchronize drift when manifest is stale.

Operational behavior
- `cargo` is an alias of `inventory` (last known manifest snapshot).
- `cargo audit` queues manifest reconciliation job.

Usage notes
- Run audit after salvage/install operations if values look outdated.

Live fuel telemetry
- inventory shows antimatter in equivalent capsules directly from the propulsion meter, independent of the scrap/module manifest. cargo audit never resets fuel.

Related commands
- `inventory`, `inventory update`, `modules`, `jobs`
