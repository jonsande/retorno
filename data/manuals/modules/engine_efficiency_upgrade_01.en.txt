MODULE DOSSIER — engine_efficiency_upgrade_01

Role
- Ship engine metering and thrust-control efficiency package.

Operational effect
- Multiplies interstellar antimatter cost by 0.8: 20% less consumption at unchanged integrity and selected speed, including the speed-tier multiplier.
- Does not repair engines or change their effective maximum integrity, travel speed or arrival time.
- Local travel remains free of capsule consumption. Multiple copies do not compound the improvement.

Integration notes
- Requires a cargo copy, 18 scrap and a deployed shipboard drone with battery and drone_bay support.
- drone install <drone_id> engine_efficiency_upgrade_01 activates the effect when installation completes.
- drone uninstall <drone_id> engine_efficiency_upgrade_01 returns a copy to cargo; the modifier disappears when the last copy is removed.
- Compare nav map routes before and after installation; inspect with module inspect engine_efficiency_upgrade_01.
- Fuel already charged for an active transit is unchanged by installation or removal.
- engine_speed_upgrade_01 can be installed simultaneously. It enables upgraded speed independently; this efficiency package only changes consumption.
