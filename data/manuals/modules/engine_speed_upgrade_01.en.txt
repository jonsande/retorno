MODULE DOSSIER — engine_speed_upgrade_01

Role
- Ship engine cruise-speed package, independent of the antimatter efficiency package.

Operational effect
- Raises the cruise ceiling to 0.8c at nominal integrity, 0.6c at LIMITED and 0.4c at DAMAGED. Without this package the respective ceilings are 0.5c, 0.4c and 0.3c. UPGRADED is shown only with nominal integrity.
- Wear reduces the ceiling; repair recovers it while installed. Critical/offline engines block departures. An explicit target above a reduced ceiling stays selected and blocks the next interstellar departure until repaired or reconfigured.
- No direct fuel change at the same selected speed/integrity; no integrity, maximum-integrity or electrical effect. With engines speed max, the higher speed increases fuel cost according to its speed tier. Copies do not stack.
- Local travel keeps its independent 20 km/s speed and existing engine requirements.
- Each interstellar departure fixes cruise speed, arrival time and the speed used by onboard clocks for that whole journey. Later wear, repairs or module changes affect future departures.

Integration notes
- Requires a cargo copy, 18 scrap and a deployed shipboard drone with battery and drone_bay support.
- drone install <drone_id> engine_speed_upgrade_01 activates the package when installation completes.
- drone uninstall <drone_id> engine_speed_upgrade_01 returns a copy to cargo. Removing the last copy restores NOMINAL if integrity allows, or the applicable damaged state.
- engine_efficiency_upgrade_01 can remain installed simultaneously; it changes only antimatter cost.
- Compare nav map routes and diag engines; status distinguishes available speed from actual transit speed.
- Installation and removal retain ordinary drone task restrictions, including restrictions on requesting new tasks during transit.
- Query/select with engines speed, engines speed max or engines speed 0.3; consult man engine_speed. A fixed target is not lowered silently when the last copy is removed.
