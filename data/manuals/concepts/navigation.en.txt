SHIP OS — Navigation & Route Knowledge (Field Copy)
Ref: OS-MAN/ENG/NAV-01

Navigation runs on partial knowledge, not a complete galaxy map.
RETORNO combines:
- known nodes/contacts,
- known links/routes,
- local telemetry.

Navigation blocks
- Global topology: `nav map graph [node_id]`.
- Known pathing: `nav map path <node_id>`.
- Direct routes from current position: `nav map routes`.
- Known contacts from current position: `nav map contacts`.
- Known world sectors: `nav map sectors`.
- Galactic situational view (physical layer): `nav map galaxy [sector|local|regional|global]`.
- Transit: `nav <node_id>`, `nav --no-cruise <node_id>`, `nav abort`.

How route knowledge is acquired
- `uplink` on docked relay/station/waystation.
- `route solve` with sufficient sensors + power.
- `scan` for local discovery.
- `intel import` from external logs/fragments.

Relays
- Relays keep and repeat old/partial tables.
- They can expand knowledge, but data freshness is not guaranteed.

Coordinates vs IDs
- `sector`: S{sx}_{sy}_{sz} grid cell from x/y/z.
- `node_id`: operational identifier used by `nav`, `dock`, `ship survey`.

Public location IDs
- Contacts, maps and acquired navigation records show a compact location ID such as P-1.2.0.1. It is a single token: copy it directly or use TAB.
- Use that ID with nav, route solve, dock, ship survey, nav map path/graph and drone location targets. Existing named installation IDs and full archived IDs remain valid.
- TAB offers targets appropriate to the command: known contacts, known routes or the current local installation. A shared prefix lists choices; it never selects a destination for you.
- IDs persist across saves within this voyage. They do not establish a route, present activity or permission to access an unknown installation.
- Read an acquired navigation record to register its references. The display abbreviates installation references; the retained archive and its integrity data are preserved.

Operational notes
- Interstellar departure speed follows engine condition. engine_speed_upgrade_01 permits UPGRADED with nominal integrity; engine_efficiency_upgrade_01 independently reduces capsule consumption. See man engines and both module dossiers.
- Cruise is inertial: speed, ETA and the speed used by onboard clocks are fixed at departure. Engine changes affect future departures. Local speed stays at 20 km/s.
- status and diag engines show available and actual transit speed; nav map routes and confirmation include galactic duration estimates.
- Knowing a contact is not the same as knowing a route to it.
- `nav map *` depends on `core_os` only (not on `sensors` or `data_core`).
- Region semantics (bulge/disk/halo) are evaluated on the physical galactic layer.
- `nav map galaxy` uses four fixed radii: `10ly`, `100ly`, `10000ly`, `500000ly`.
- Single-hop travel and route publication are guarded by `MAX_ROUTE_HOP_LY` (default `45ly`).
- `ship survey <target>` gives point telemetry for known contacts and requires active `sensors` + `data_core`.
- An unknown-contact rejection does not establish whether the destination physically exists. Discover it through scan or acquired intel.

Structural navigation notices
- A legitimate local transmission may offer an onward route when known exploration opportunities are exhausted. Enable comms and radiod at its source, then read/import the received file.
- Each notice describes one leg. Explore its destination to obtain the next broadcast or recoverable record. All legs obey the 45 ly limit; resources and repairs remain your responsibility.
- Captured notices and registered routes are different records. status reports receiver availability, not undetected traffic or onward opportunities. No exit is guaranteed.

Relevant commands
- `nav`, `nav galaxy`, `ship survey`, `scan`, `route solve`, `uplink`, `intel import`, `debug galaxy map <scale>`, `dock`, `undock`.
