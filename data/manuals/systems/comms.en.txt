SYSTEM SUMMARY — comms
Ref: OS-MAN/SYS/COMMS

The Communications System receives passive radio traffic independently of the exploration sensors and data uplink stack.

Function
- Automatically captures physically available signals and station broadcasts into readable files.
- Records captured messages for subsequent reading/import.

Operational dependencies
- Initial hardware condition: OFFLINE, health 0.72, electrically disconnected with no load.
- Initial health supports LIMITED operation after power on comms. Repair is needed before activation only if health falls below 0.60.
- Hardware in BRG-01; energy_distribution >= nominal and effective electrical supply.
- Power on comms, then boot radiod (Radio Communications Service; 5 seconds).
- No sensors, sensord, data_core or datad dependency for passive radio.
- Reception is suspended during transit, hibernation, terminal lock or insufficient power.

Command impact by state
- LIMITED, NOMINAL and UPGRADED can receive with radiod running; LIMITED has lower sensitivity, UPGRADED higher sensitivity than NOMINAL.
- DAMAGED, CRITICAL and OFFLINE cannot receive.
- Nominal load: 0.15 kW; LIMITED: 0.12 kW. diag comms reports actual state and load.
- Internal mail and salvage do not require radio. Uplink retains data_core/datad requirements.

Common symptoms
- No newly captured signal; silence does not establish the absence of emitters.
- radiod stopped, uninstalled or power dependency unavailable.
- status and diag comms describe the receiver and service; they do not enumerate undetected signals.

Recovery pattern
- Inspect diag comms and stabilize electrical distribution.
- Repair damaged comms with a drone in BRG-01 when required.
- power on comms; boot radiod. Power activation does not start the service.
- Remain within physical coverage; lore triggered by docking still requires docking.
- radiod listens continuously without repeated scan. Reception needs sufficient signal strength, which falls with distance, and a wave that has arrived.
- Light takes one year to cross one light year. Old automatic transmissions may already be arriving; reception does not prove present activity or living senders.
- Continuous transmissions can be captured later while available; a brief transmission missed with the radio off may be lost.
- Knowing a route does not prove coverage. A station may have no active transmitter. Local documents triggered by docking still require their original interaction.
- Read the received file with cat or mail read to apply valid intel.

- Structural navigation notices identify one reachable stage at a time. Read the captured file and reach that source before expecting its next notice or salvage record.
- An exhausted station with a legitimate emission may continue providing its transmissions; this does not refill supplies or recovered files. No reception guarantees a safe onward journey.

Related commands
- diag comms, power on comms, power off comms, boot radiod, jobs
- drone deploy D1 BRG-01, drone repair D1 comms, cat <path>, intel import <path>
