SYSTEM SUMMARY — comms
Ref: OS-MAN/SYS/COMMS

The Communications System receives passive radio traffic independently of the exploration sensors and data uplink stack.

Function
- Captures locally available signals and station broadcasts into readable files.
- Separates pending reception, captured messages and subsequent reading/import.

Operational dependencies
- Hardware in BRG-01; energy_distribution >= nominal and effective electrical supply.
- Power on comms, then boot radiod (Radio Communications Service; 5 seconds).
- No sensors, sensord, data_core or datad dependency for passive radio.
- Reception is suspended during transit, hibernation, terminal lock or insufficient power.

Command impact by state
- LIMITED, NOMINAL and UPGRADED can receive with radiod running.
- DAMAGED, CRITICAL and OFFLINE cannot receive.
- Nominal load: 0.15 kW; LIMITED: 0.12 kW. diag comms reports actual state and load.
- Internal mail and salvage do not require radio. Uplink retains data_core/datad requirements.

Common symptoms
- No newly captured signal despite a pending communication.
- radiod stopped, uninstalled, power dependency unavailable or source outside local reception conditions.
- Pending counts do not reveal message contents or destinations.

Recovery pattern
- Inspect diag comms and stabilize electrical distribution.
- Repair damaged comms with a drone in BRG-01 when required.
- power on comms; boot radiod. Power activation does not start the service.
- Remain at the legitimate source; lore triggered by docking still requires docking.
- Pending reception retries automatically while its source remains locally available.
- Read the received file with cat or mail read to apply valid intel.

Related commands
- diag comms, power on comms, power off comms, boot radiod, jobs
- drone deploy D1 BRG-01, drone repair D1 comms, cat <path>, intel import <path>
