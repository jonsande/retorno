SYSTEM SUMMARY — engines
Ref: OS-MAN/SYS/ENG-R1

RETORNO's antimatter engines occupy ENG-R1. One integrity reading covers the whole assembly.

Function
- The antimatter feeder transfers and meters capsule contents under confinement.
- The annihilation chamber combines controlled quantities of ordinary matter and antimatter, managing heat and radiation.
- The propulsion assembly directs reaction energy and products through magnetic guidance and a magnetic nozzle to produce sublight thrust.
- Ordinary reactants come from internal reserves; they require no separate operator inventory.

Operational dependencies
- Electrical auxiliaries draw 0.2 kW at NOMINAL. Automatic CRUISE and load shedding preserve their supply.
- power_core is an independent fusion electricity source; it does not consume these capsules.
- CRG-01 stores capsules with autonomous electromagnetic traps and high vacuum, independent of the main grid.
- Navigation still requires a known direct route, a hop within 45 ly, valid ship power and Core OS, and an undocked ship.

Command impact by state
- Standard departure speeds: UPGRADED 0.8c, NOMINAL 0.5c, LIMITED 0.4c, DAMAGED 0.3c. UPGRADED requires engine_speed_upgrade_01 and nominal integrity; repair restores the improvement while installed.
- Every interstellar journey freezes its speed and arrival time at departure; onboard clocks keep using that speed despite later engine damage, repairs, shutdown or module changes. Local travel stays at 20 km/s.
- status and diag engines distinguish available departure speed from actual transit speed; nav map routes and confirmation include estimated galactic duration.
- OFFLINE, forced off or CRITICAL: departure blocked. Use power on engines or repair as indicated by diag engines.
- DAMAGED or better: departure allowed when other requirements are met.
- Wear reduces efficiency continuously, including within a state band; repair recovers efficiency.
- Local travel consumes no antimatter. For interstellar departures the current estimate is:
  cost = (0.5 + distance_ly / 10) * (1 + 1.5 * (1 - normalized_integrity)^2) * upgrade_multiplier.
- normalized_integrity is integrity divided by effective maximum, limited to 0..1.
- upgrade_multiplier is 1.0, or 0.8 with engine_efficiency_upgrade_01 installed.
- nav map routes shows estimated capsule equivalents and available reserve for known direct routes. Navigation confirmation repeats the estimate.
- Insufficient fuel blocks departure without changing navigation or consuming fuel. Accepted departure charges once; nav abort gives no refund and adds no charge.
- New voyage reserve: 12.0 equivalent capsules. Fractions are retained; subsequent wear affects future departures, not fuel already charged.

Common symptoms
- Rising estimates for the same route indicate reduced integrity or removal of the efficiency module.
- Electrical isolation, critical damage or exhausted reserves prevent departure.

Recovery pattern
- Read diag engines, locate engines and power status.
- Deploy a drone to ENG-R1; drone repair <drone_id> engines uses ordinary repair materials and restores global integrity.
- Restore electrical supply if isolated; compare nav map routes after repair.
- Installing engine_efficiency_upgrade_01 reduces consumption by 20% at the same integrity. Removing it restores the unmodified cost; repeated copies do not compound this improvement.
- The terminal reports global engine integrity; separate component diagnostics are unavailable.

Related commands
- diag engines, locate engines, power on engines, power status, inventory
- drone deploy <drone_id> ENG-R1, drone repair <drone_id> engines
- nav map routes, nav <node_id>, nav abort, man engine_efficiency_upgrade_01, man engine_speed_upgrade_01
