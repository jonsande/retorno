At absolute time `1681353131.521778 s`, one galactic ULP is `2.384185791015625e-7 s`; the calculated proper limit is `1.3829e-7 s`. Inverting through absolute clocks rounded it to the current instant. After the 50% charge crossing, regulation reverses the electrical rate and another real positive limit lies about `6.05e-9 s` later. The coordinator rejected these limits because it could not represent them on the large absolute galactic clock. A later reproduction found cancellation when subtracting large proper clocks to search for a radiological crossing; a sequence of microticks near the strict `0.85` health threshold arose from rounding the local duration.

R2-A uses the saved local segment coordinate, computes proper-time differences directly, and places a real sub-ULP cut at the next representable instant. Both clocks advance; `nextafter` selects the correct side of strict classifier thresholds. Coincident causes are grouped only when close at **both absolute and local scales**, so four ULP at a `1e16 s` clock cannot merge events seconds apart. The engine's 15% threshold is snapped exactly within the tolerance already used for other health thresholds. Only a representational remainder of up to four ULP at the end of an already advanced request is discarded. Earlier physical limits retain priority, and a real failure preserves the previous subinterval. The runtime catches unexpected exceptions, pauses automatic advancement, and emits `BOOT_BLOCKED` with details rather than silently retrying failed ticks.

R2-B profiling showed that nominal cruise forecasting cloned the full state unnecessarily; two deep copies cost a median `0.0983 s` per PT001-04 advance. Seven-day hibernation multiplied this cost over hundreds of blocks. The nominal forecast now reads state without cloning when no job or pending transition requires one. A physical tick retains a complete transactional clone through local serialization, preserving rollback and owner identity. The universe rules key is reused only within the tick's atomic transaction. Hibernation blocks may reach 26 weeks only in stable cruise with a full nondepleting battery and no jobs, recovery, pending narrative work, or drone maintenance effects. Other states retain the seven-day horizon. Each causal boundary returns to the caller for the next choice. No subsystem was disabled; radio retains its preexisting suspended reception rule during hibernation.

R2-C diagnosis confirmed `_run_hibernate_start_sequence()` called synchronous processing on Textual's event loop. Processing now runs in a worker; a queue transfers real simulated time to Textual's thread for widget updates. Input remains disabled throughout the sequence; panels and automatic events are read in one snapshot under a nonblocking lock, skipping a frame when the engine is busy. `on_unmount` waits for the worker before saving. Existing audio, countdown, and wakeup presentation remain. An initial `on_shutdown` handler was found not to receive Textual's close event and was corrected during validation.
